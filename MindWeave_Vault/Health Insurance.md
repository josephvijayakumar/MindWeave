# Health Insurance

Having a personal family floater or individual health insurance policy is crucial, separate from corporate insurance, to cover unexpected medical bills. Key terms include PPC (Pre-Policy Check Up) which is a medical screening mandated before policy approval, and Restoration Benefits, which automatically refill an exhausted insurance cover for future, usually unrelated, claims within the same year.
