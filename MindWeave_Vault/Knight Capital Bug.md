# Knight Capital Bug

In 2012, Knight Capital lost $440 million in 45 minutes due to a deployment error. Old test code ('Power Peg') meant to buy high and sell low was left in the system, and a new deployment missed 1 out of 8 servers, triggering the dormant code and causing 4 million erroneous trades that bankrupted the company.
